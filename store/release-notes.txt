First release.

A time bank ledger for a group that already meets. One hour given earns one hour, and an hour is an hour
whoever gives it. Balances are worked out from the book every time you look, and a ledger line is written
only when both people say the hour happened.

Everyone starts at plus two and the node carries them to minus three. Export the book and hand it to
another keeper, who can merge it.

No account, no ads, no internet permission.
